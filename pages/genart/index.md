Each series is a small program. Every piece comes from one hash, so the same hash always gives the same artwork at any size. Open a series to browse its selected pieces, roll new seeds and share the exact piece you like. Two posts walk through the series with live demos: [the first fourteen](/posts/genart) and [eighteen more](/posts/genart-series-2).
